# Report Page Specifications

1. **Executive Overview** — revenue, cost, gross profit/margin, shipments, budget variance, freshness, and failed-quality banner; monthly trend and country contribution.
2. **Country Performance** — country KPI matrix, margin ranking, month trend, and controlled tooltip explaining FX/reporting currency.
3. **Customer and Route Profitability** — customer/route scatter and ranked table with revenue, cost, margin, shipment count; cross-filtering and detail navigation.
4. **Budget vs Actual** — cost-center/month variance waterfall, variance matrix, favorable/unfavorable formatting, and missing-budget indicator.
5. **Cost Drivers and FX Impact** — cost-type decomposition, local-vs-EUR comparison, FX impact trend, and rate context.
6. **Data Quality and Refresh Status** — latest successful refresh, failed rows/amount by rule and dataset, batch history, reconciliation status, and steward-friendly reason details.
7. **Drill-through Transaction Detail** — invoice/shipment/cost identifiers, dates, dimensions, local amount/currency, FX rate, EUR amount, and batch lineage; export governed by role.

Every page shows the active date/country context and a reporting-currency label. KPI cards drill through or provide a tooltip that states grain and exclusions.
